Power-Ups Guide

❤️ Extra Life
Gives +1 life (max 3 hearts).
Spawns from score 25+.
• Normal / Tiny / Chunky / Endless: ~5.1% chance per pipe
• Power-Up Frenzy: ~35% chance
• Wonks Awakening: ~4% chance
• Hardcore: does NOT spawn

🍌 Bananas
Awards +5 Bananas used to purchase items from the Banana Shop.
Spawns from score 25+.
• Normal / Tiny / Chunky / Endless / Frenzy: ~2.5% chance per pipe
• Wonks Awakening: ~5.5% chance
• Hardcore: rare ~1.5% chance

👻 Ghost
Ghost mode ~12.5 seconds: pass through pipes safely.
Spawns from score 25+ (except Hardcore).
• Normal / Tiny / Chunky / Endless: ~0.4% chance
• Power-Up Frenzy: ~15% chance
• Wonks Awakening: ~1.2% chance

🔥 Fire Power
~12.5 seconds of faster pipes, red trail, higher gravity, +2 score per pipe (stacks with Glitch to +4), pass through pipes safely.
• Power-Up Frenzy: ~8% chance
• Wonks Awakening: ~11% chance
• All other modes: never appears

⚡ Glitch
~12.5 seconds of screen glitches, faster pipes, +2 score per pipe (stacks with Fire to +4).
Spawns from score 25+ (except Hardcore & Wonks).
• Normal / Tiny / Chunky / Endless: ~0.4% chance
• Power-Up Frenzy: ~12% chance
• Wonks Awakening & Hardcore: never appears

🍔 Food Power-Ups (Cosmetic)

🥤 Soda • 🍯 Honey • 🍿 Popcorn • 🍬 Candy Cane • 🍦 Ice Cream • 🍩 Donut • 🍫 Chocolate • 🍔 Burger • 🍕 Pizza
These special food power-ups spawn in every mode after score 25.
They are very rare (~0.6% chance per pipe).

Collecting 10 of the same type unlocks a special Monkey skin + matching title.
Once you unlock the skin, that specific power-up will stop spawning for you.

📈 XP & Leveling System

Gain XP to level up and track your progress!

• +1 XP — every pipe you pass
• +50 XP — every time your score reaches a new multiple of 50
• +10 XP — every Banana collected
• +5 XP — every other power-up collected
• +15 XP — every Gift collected
• +30–50 XP — every Egg collected
• +200 XP — bonus for winning Normal mode
• +50 XP — every achievement unlocked!

XP is saved forever — keep playing to level up higher!

🏆 Achievements System

+50 XP for every achievement you complete!

Unlock achievements by playing, collecting power-ups, hitting high scores, and exploring every mode. They are all tracked forever and shown in your Profile menu.

🛒 Shop Items Explained

Extra Life Token — +1 starting life next game (max 4 hearts)
Banana Doubler Ticket — ×2 Bananas from all 🍌 for 1 game
Score Booster — +8 score per pipe (stacks with Fire/Glitch)
Pipe Skins, Title FX, Profile Backgrounds — permanent cosmetics
